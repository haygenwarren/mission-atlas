# Mission list for v1

Draft of the 100 missions to load before launch. Edit freely: delete a line to
drop a mission, add a line to include one, and keep the total near 100.

**Scope:** robotic missions beyond Earth orbit for planetary science, plus
space-based astrophysics observatories. Failures are included. Crewed
missions, Earth science and Sun-focused missions are out, though they will eventually be included.

Each line is: name · launch year · agencies · why it is on the list ·
catalogue designator. Every designator below was confirmed in GCAT
(J. McDowell, planet4589.org). Agencies are a first pass and get checked when
each mission is entered.

**(pilot)** marks the ten proposed for the first batch.
